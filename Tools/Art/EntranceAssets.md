# Entrance menu artwork

## Blackout stairwell v5

Generated from scratch with the built-in imagegen tool, not edited from v3/v4. Saved as `Assets/Art/UI/Entrance/MenuBackgroundSourceV5Night.png` and assigned to the menu. Direction: exhausted arrival at a freezing, unpowered apartment entrance at 3 AM, rare weak light and the old Source screenshot aesthetic. Earlier variants are preserved.

### Prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: NEW full-bleed 16:9 PC horror-game menu background.
This is a NEW composition, NOT a brightness edit of an earlier image.

PRIMARY FEELING:
It is 3 AM. You just stumbled into your familiar Soviet apartment entrance, exhausted after drinking, expecting ordinary safety. The electricity is out. The air is unnaturally freezing and the familiar space suddenly feels wrong. The deep black corridor makes your skin crawl. Show the environment from that tired person's first-person viewpoint, without showing any person or body parts. Quiet oppressive everyday horror, not spectacular destruction.

VISUAL STYLE IS CRITICAL:
Make it look like a raw gameplay screenshot from the ORIGINAL 2004 Half-Life 2 Source engine: simple low-poly BSP brush geometry, coarse flat photo-sourced diffuse textures at 256/512px, repeated dirty wall and floor textures, simple angular metal railings and doors, modest antialiasing, broad low-resolution baked lightmap shadows. Extremely plain dated real-time videogame rendering. Absolutely NOT photographic realism, NOT modern concept art, NOT Unreal Engine, NOT cinematic lighting, NOT glossy detailed AI illustration.

SCENE / NEW LAYOUT:
A cramped, familiar post-Soviet residential stairwell entrance at night. The viewpoint stands just inside a heavy entrance door, looking diagonally across a small landing. A low stair flight disappears up into blackness at the far right. A small high dirty stairwell window and the lower wall are barely visible near the middle-right. A narrow corridor to closed apartment doors retreats into almost pitch-black darkness behind a concrete column. A few dull square mailboxes, one tired radiator and simple exposed heating pipes. Peeling grey plaster with sickly dark green paint below, dry scuffed tile floor. Human-scale, mundane, cramped, simple, with a low ceiling.

LIGHTING MUST BE RADICALLY DIFFERENT FROM A NORMAL LIT ROOM:
NO powered indoor lights whatsoever. The fluorescent fixture is present but completely dead and unlit. The room is about 80% deep near-black shadow. Only TWO tiny plausible sources: a weak, cold blue-grey night glow leaking through the small dirty window, and a razor-thin sickly warm line under one far apartment door. The cold window spill catches just the edge of a stair rail, a small patch of chipped wall and a few floor tiles; it does NOT fill the room. Most doors, the ceiling and the corridor merge into darkness. Hard contrast between a couple of small dim light patches and large solid shadow masses, as a Source level baked with almost zero ambient light. No sunlight, no moonbeam spotlight, no visible light rays, no volumetric fog. Cold stagnant air must be conveyed through colour, darkness and architecture.
Deep indigo-grey / dirty green-black shadows, a very restrained desaturated blue patch and the tiny distant amber door crack. Preserve faint readable silhouettes of architecture so it is not a blank black image.

COMPOSITION FOR THE EXISTING MENU:
1920x1080 landscape composition. Slightly imperfect ordinary eye-level in-game viewpoint, subtle unease, no camera blur or overt Dutch angle. Lower-left third stays almost black and uncluttered for large ivory menu choices. Upper-right quarter stays dark and quiet for overlaid poster text. Architectural interest and the isolated tiny pools of light are around centre-right. Keep focal features within central 90% for 16:10 cropping.
No people, no visible monster, no jump-scare face, no blood, no weapon, no HUD, no crosshair, no text, no logos, no border or watermark.
STRICTLY A HUMBLE OLD SOURCE GAME SCREENSHOT: dry matte surfaces, low-detail props, simple boxy geometry, grainy diffuse textures. No wet floor, puddles, reflections, bloom, lens effects, depth of field, film grading, rich photographic microdetail or modern renderer polish.
```

```text

Change ONLY the lighting and atmosphere: make this same scene significantly darker, gloomier and more uneasy, as if the old Source level had been compiled with much lower ambient light. The distant corridor should become very dark; leave the distant weak fixture and a faintly readable far door as a small ominous focal point. Keep the ordinary flat baked-lightmap look of a 2004 Source engine screenshot. Maintain dry matte surfaces, coarse diffuse textures, simple low-poly BSP geometry, original perspective, composition, all props, every object position, and framing.
Do not redesign the scene or add any objects, people, monsters, particles, fog, cinematic volumetric beams, bloom, lens effects, glossy surfaces, puddles, wet reflections, photographic detail, AI concept-art polish, text, logos, UI, black borders or a heavy vignette. ## Source screenshot variant v3
```

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Saved as `Assets/Art/UI/Entrance/MenuBackgroundSourceV3.png` and assigned to the menu. Earlier versions are preserved. The user requested a vintage Source screenshot appearance rather than photorealistic concept art.

### Prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: PC game menu background, landscape 16:9, 1920x1080 composition.
Create an ORIGINAL scene that looks unmistakably like an actual raw gameplay screenshot rendered inside the 2004 Half-Life 2 Source engine. This is a deliberately plain, dated videogame screenshot, NOT realistic artwork, NOT a movie still, NOT a modern Unreal render, NOT a concept painting.

SCENE: a small shabby Soviet apartment building entrance hallway leading into a stairwell. Dirty off-white walls with worn dark green lower paint, six or eight rectangular metal mailboxes, one flat brown apartment door, a blocky radiator and a few simple exposed pipes. Concrete stairs and angular metal railings at far right, a rectangular doorway to a darker back corridor near center-right. Human scale, ordinary dull believable architecture, abandoned and unsettling through emptiness.

RENDERING IS THE MAIN REQUIREMENT:
Authentic stock Half-Life 2 / Source 2004 visual grammar: low polygon BSP brush geometry; very straight boxy architecture; coarse low-resolution 512x512 diffuse textures repeated across broad surfaces; noticeably blurry, flat photo-sourced wall textures with baked grime; simple low-detail props and visibly angular silhouettes; largely diffuse materials with extremely restrained specular. Static lightmaps with broad soft-edged lighting and dark ambient corners. Limited lighting range, flat midtones, faint yellow-green indoor color cast. Simple fluorescent fixture that barely glows. Modest videogame antialiasing and slightly crunchy pixel edges. A scene that could genuinely run on a mid-2000s PC and be captured with a screenshot key.
Dry matte floor with repeating dirty concrete tile texture. Painted surfaces do not glisten. Grime must be contained in the diffuse texture, not millions of tiny sculpted cracks or modern physically simulated detail. Door geometry simple and flat. Broad rectangular lightmap shadows, not cinematic shafts.
Absolutely NO modern photographic realism, film grading, volumetric fog, bloom, dramatic depth of field, rim lighting, lens flare, glossy wet floors, puddles, mirror reflections, path tracing, ray tracing, ultra-detailed rust, intricate wiring, moody concept-art brushwork, oversharpening, photographic microdetail, cinematic composition, or expensive 3D render look.

COMPOSITION: ordinary eye-level first-person in-game camera, approximately 75 degree horizontal FOV, no clever film angle. Slightly asymmetrical everyday layout, clear vanishing point down the hallway. Keep lower-left reasonably dark and plain for menu buttons, and upper-right not busy for overlaid large text. The whole image should read as a humble eerie game LEVEL rather than a photograph of an abandoned building.
No HUD, weapon, crosshair, people, monsters, text, logos, buttons or watermarks. No copied screenshot; original room and layout, strictly vintage Source-engine appearance.
```

## Button prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: single isolated horizontal button background sprite for a realistic Soviet stairwell horror game menu. Exactly ONE wide rectangular worn painted metal apartment intercom nameplate, flat front orthographic view, aspect ratio 5:1 with modest transparent padding around it. Dark charcoal olive metal face, finely scratched edges, subtle tarnished brass border and tiny screw heads in four corners, restrained old industrial craftsmanship, empty clean center dark enough for readable light UI text. Photoreal texture, understated premium game UI, no perspective, no shadow outside silhouette, no text, letters, symbols, labels, additional buttons or mockup. Actual transparent background. Deliver one centered sprite filling the image horizontally.

The main-menu invitation uses **Russo One** in uppercase for a bold industrial poster style. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/russoone. Its SIL Open Font License is included as `Assets/Art/UI/Entrance/Fonts/RussoOne-OFL.txt`.

Noto Sans Regular, Noto Serif Bold and Noto Serif Italic are also included with their license beside the font files. Interface text is rendered in Unity, so button labels and the run clock stay sharp and editable.
